## Summary

Adds `services/relayer/`, a Node/TypeScript gasless fee-bump relayer for GridPay:

- `POST /v1/relayer/sponsor` accepts a customer-signed Soroban transaction XDR and `GET /healthz` for probes.
- Strict policy engine: only `InvokeHostFunction` operations targeting whitelisted GridPay contract IDs and approved entrypoints (`create_payment`, `pay_installment`, `authorize_trial_conversion_amount`, `claim_rebate`, `approve_release`, ...) are allowed. `Payment`, `ChangeTrust`, `SetOptions`, `AccountMerge`, nested fee bumps, reverting simulations, restore-required transactions and fees above `MAX_SPONSORED_STROOPS` are rejected.
- Simulates the inner transaction against Soroban RPC before signing; enforces atomic per-customer and per-merchant daily quotas in Redis with reservations released on any later failure.
- Wraps valid transactions in a fee bump, signs via an internal KMS endpoint (no private keys in the service), submits through Soroban RPC, polls for confirmation, retries once on `tx_bad_seq`, and halts with an SRE alert when the fee-source balance drops below the configured floor.
- `ChannelPool` leases fee-source accounts exclusively with a FIFO wait queue and bounded timeout; sequence state advances on success and refreshes from RPC on failure.
- Structured JSONL accounting log records exact stroop spend (inner fee, total fee, incremental) with customer, merchant, contract IDs and entrypoints for merchant rebate / relayer stipend reconciliation.
- Wires the service into `make relayer-test` and a dedicated CI job; documents API, security model and configuration in `services/relayer/README.md`.
